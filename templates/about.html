About AutoWorth

A little more confidence
for your next drive.

We turn real-world used-car listings into a simple, useful starting point for your next decision.

THE IDEA

Make the car market
feel a little simpler.

Pricing a pre-owned car can feel like guesswork. AutoWorth brings together a compact listing dataset and a machine-learning model to help you explore a reasonable estimate based on the details of your car.

Enter the make, model, year, fuel type and kilometres driven. The model looks at those characteristics together and returns an estimate in Indian rupees.
